SkyReview terms of use

Last updated 29 September 2026

These terms apply when you use SkyReview. By creating an account or posting a review you agree to them. They are short — please read the community rules in particular.

Community rules

  • Review flights you actually took, honestly.
  • Be respectful: no hate speech, harassment, threats, bullying or discrimination.
  • No sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content — in text or photos.
  • No spam, advertising, or other people's personal information.
  • Only post photos you took or have the right to share.
  • SkyReview has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Offensive language is refused automatically.

Reporting and removal

  • Anyone can report a review from its ⋯ menu, or block a person so their reviews no longer appear. Reviews reported by several people are hidden automatically.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and ban the accounts that posted it.

Your account

  • You must be at least 16 to use SkyReview. You sign in with Google (or Apple) and are responsible for what is posted from your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account.

Your content

  • Your reviews and photos stay yours. By posting them you allow SkyReview to show them to other people in the app and on our services.
  • Reviews are personal opinions of travellers, not the views of SkyReview.

Flight information

  • Schedules, aircraft types and ratings are provided for information only and may be incomplete or out of date. Always check with your airline.
  • SkyReview is provided "as is", without warranties, to the extent the law allows.

Changes

  • If we change these terms, we will update them here and change the date at the top.
