SkyReview privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

SkyReview lets travellers read and write reviews of their flights. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and only collect what the app needs to work.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google (or Apple), we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We create a SkyReview profile with your name, photo and, if you add one, a short bio.
  • Your reviews: the flight you reviewed (airline, route, flight number, aircraft), your star rating, text and any photos you add, and when you posted it.
  • Your activity: reviews you mark as helpful, reviews you report (and the reason), and people you block.
  • On your device only: the last airport you explored and short-lived cached flight schedules, stored locally to make the app faster.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment details, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What is public

  • Reviews are public: anyone using SkyReview can see your review together with your name, profile photo and how many reviews you have written.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

How we use it

  • To show reviews, ratings, rankings and your profile (including flyer levels and points).
  • To keep the community safe: reports help us hide reviews that break the rules, and blocking hides reviews from people you choose.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us run SkyReview

  • Supabase (database, accounts, photo storage) — hosted in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Google and Apple — only to sign you in, when you choose them.
  • AeroDataBox (via RapidAPI) — provides flight schedules. We only send airport codes, dates and flight numbers; no personal information.
  • Apple Maps — shows the Explore map.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account and reviews until you delete them.
  • Delete a review at any time from its ⋯ menu. Delete your whole account from Profile → Delete account: this permanently removes your profile, reviews, photos, helpful votes, reports and blocks.

Your rights

  • You can access, correct and delete your information: edit your bio and reviews in the app, or delete your account there.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to object, restrict processing, data portability and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us for anything you cannot do in the app.

Children

  • SkyReview is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

  • If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
